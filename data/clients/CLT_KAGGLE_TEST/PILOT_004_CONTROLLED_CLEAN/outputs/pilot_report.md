# Payroll Integrity Pilot Review

**Organisation:** Test Org  
**Pilot:** PILOT_004_CONTROLLED_CLEAN  
**Review Period:** 15 Mar 2024 to 10 Apr 2024  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A targeted payroll diagnostics review was conducted using available payroll data to identify triggered findings across key payroll integrity and evidence-related risk areas.

**Total Findings Identified:** **14**

### Severity Breakdown

- High: 5
- Medium: 9
- Low: 0

### Interpretation

14 findings were identified, including 5 HIGH severity items. HIGH severity findings are present but do not dominate the overall distribution.

Findings distribution: 5 HIGH, 9 MEDIUM, 0 LOW (Total: 14).

The findings profile is primarily concentrated in logical items.

Assessment coverage is partial. Some risk areas may require additional data to fully evaluate.

### Recommended Focus

Prioritise review of HIGH severity findings, followed by broader validation of related processes and data inputs.

## 2. Why This Matters

This pilot review is designed to surface triggered findings from the supplied payroll data so they can be validated and prioritised for follow-up.

- Triggered findings may indicate process weaknesses, data inconsistencies, or evidentiary gaps
- Higher-severity findings should be reviewed first to confirm root cause and operational impact
- Lower-severity findings can still highlight control weaknesses worth addressing over time

This pilot is intended to provide an initial evidence-based view of observed issues within the supplied data, not a full assurance conclusion.

## 3. Sample Findings

*This pilot includes a limited selection of findings to demonstrate the types of issues identified.*

### Finding 1 — Termination processing

- **Rule:** TERM-010
- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Example employee:** E002

**Issue**
Payroll activity was recorded after the employee termination date beyond the allowed tolerance window.

**Suggested Action**
Review the termination date and subsequent payroll activity to confirm whether the employee was correctly finalised.

### Finding 2 — Leave and entitlement integrity

- **Rule:** LEAVE-007
- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Example employee:** E002

**Issue**
Leave transactions were recorded after the employee termination date.

**Suggested Action**
Review termination processing and correct any invalid leave transactions.

### Finding 3 — Cross-module integrity

- **Rule:** CM-006
- **Severity:** HIGH
- **Example employee:** E002

**Issue**
Payroll activity and leave ledger movement both continue after the employee termination date.

**Suggested Action**
Review the employee termination timeline, payroll activity, and leave transactions to confirm whether the employee was correctly finalised.

These examples represent a subset of the findings identified and are intended to illustrate the types of triggered conditions present rather than the full extent of issues across the review.

## 4. Scope & Methodology

This review was conducted using structured payroll data extracts and rule-based diagnostics.

- No system access was required
- Analysis focused on payroll transactions and derived outcomes
- Findings reflect triggered rule conditions observable within the supplied data
- All findings are derived directly from supplied payroll data and system outputs

## 5. Next Steps

A full review can provide:

- complete findings across all modules
- detailed employee-level impact analysis
- root cause identification
- practical remediation guidance
- audit-ready reporting outputs
